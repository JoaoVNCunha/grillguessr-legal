Privacy Policy for Grillguessr

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Grillguessr ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Grillguessr is designed to require as little personal information as possible. We collect:

  • Anonymous account identifier. When you open the App, we create an anonymous account through Firebase Authentication. This generates a random user ID that lets us save your game progress and scores. It is not linked to your name or email unless you choose to sign in.
  • Optional sign-in information. Where account linking is available, Firebase Authentication processes your email and password to create or access your account. We use your account identifier to associate your scores with your account across devices.
  • Game data. Your scores, daily-challenge results, and leaderboard entries are stored in our database (Google Firebase Firestore).
  • Advertising data. Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads, and Meta Audience Network may collect advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, and ad interactions. See Section 3.
  • Device and diagnostic data. Standard technical information (device type, operating system version, app version, and crash data) may be collected to keep the App working reliably.

The App does not request your phone number, precise location, personal photos, or contacts. If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you send.

2. How We Use Information

  • To save and sync your game progress and scores.
  • To display leaderboards.
  • To show advertisements that help keep the App free.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve performance.

3. Advertising

The Android app uses Unity LevelPlay to obtain advertisements from Meta Audience Network and ironSource Ads. These services use advertising data to select and measure advertisements and prevent fraud. IP addresses can indicate your approximate location.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to collect privacy choices where required. When GDPR applies, the app requests ads only after consent for device storage and both advertising partners. You can change your choices through Settings > Privacy options when this option is available. The app also sends sale/sharing restriction signals to LevelPlay and requests Meta Limited Data Use where supported. These settings do not prevent all advertising data collection.

You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Google's consent service follows the Google Privacy Policy. This updated Android version does not request AdMob advertisements. Older installed versions can still use Google AdMob, which processes advertising identifiers, device information, and ad interactions.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore) — Firebase Privacy
  • Unity LevelPlay and ironSource Ads — Unity Privacy Policy
  • Meta Audience Network — Meta Privacy Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob in older versions — Google Privacy Policy

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the third-party service providers listed above, solely to operate the App and serve advertisements.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete your Firebase account or server data. To request deletion of your account and associated scores, contact us at the email below. Include your player identifier, if available, so we can locate your records.

7. Children's Privacy

Grillguessr is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
