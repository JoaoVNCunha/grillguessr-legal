Privacy Policy for Grillguessr

Last updated: 9 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Grillguessr ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Grillguessr is designed to require as little personal information as possible. We collect:

  • Anonymous account identifier. When you open the App, we create an anonymous account through Firebase Authentication. This generates a random user ID that lets us save your game progress and scores. It is not linked to your name or email unless you choose to sign in.
  • Optional sign-in information. If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we receive a unique account identifier and, where you permit it, your display name. We use this only to associate your scores with your account across devices.
  • Game data. Your scores, daily-challenge results, and leaderboard entries are stored in our database (Google Firebase Firestore).
  • Advertising identifiers. Our advertising partner (Google AdMob) may collect your device's advertising ID and related technical data to serve ads. See Section 3.
  • Device and diagnostic data. Standard technical information (device type, operating system version, app version, and crash data) may be collected to keep the App working reliably.

We do not ask for or collect your real name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, or contacts.

2. How We Use Information

  • To save and sync your game progress and scores.
  • To display leaderboards.
  • To show advertisements that help keep the App free.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve performance.

3. Advertising

Grillguessr displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and other data to serve and measure ads. By default, the App requests non-personalized ads. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the AdMob & AdSense policies. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore) — Firebase Privacy
  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign-In — Apple Privacy Policy

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the third-party service providers listed above, solely to operate the App and serve advertisements.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Your anonymous account and game data are retained while the App is installed. To request deletion of your data, contact us at the email below and we will delete your account record and associated scores.

7. Children's Privacy

Grillguessr is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
